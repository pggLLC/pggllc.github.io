# Original-site coverage audit

Compared November 26, 2026 against the original live pggllc.org homepage, /services, /about-us and /contact-us, retrieved directly before domain cutover. This comparison is against the original GoDaddy-hosted site, not the first GitHub draft.

## Honest conclusion

The first GitHub draft did not communicate all of the original firm's scope. It focused on affordable housing feasibility and entitlements and omitted much of the broader planning, facilitation, analytics, and municipal leadership offer. The redesign restores all five original service lines, restores the public-sector client context, and strengthens the evidence for the firm's depth. It is a reorganization and condensation, not verbatim parity.

## Added evidence and services

- Site & LIHTC Readiness Review, dedicated entitlement strategy, and a clearly fictional board-ready memo implement the requested housing offer without erasing the full practice.
- Erie’s official appointment announcement independently supports Paul’s interim title, prior Lyons role, education and management of more than ten comprehensive plans. The previously linked qualifications packet belongs to another firm and has been removed; it is not a pggLLC qualifications package.
- About explicitly labels this as experience across Paul's career, including prior roles and collaborations, rather than claiming every commission was performed by pggLLC.
- The Rifle/Fairbanks project examples and teaching claim have been removed pending a suitable PGG/Paul-owned source. COHO Analytics is presented as a directly attributable pggLLC analytical resource.
- Fruita Mews facts remain linked to CHFA, City of Fruita and Housing Colorado sources. The project is attributed to IndiBuild, not presented as a separate pggLLC development.
- No project photography has been invented. The custom isometric site study is labeled as an illustrative concept, not an actual property or project.

## Remaining differences / launch inputs

- Exact founding date and 30+ year claim require owner confirmation before publication.
- A direct business email and verified FirstPass public URL are still needed. The original contact page publishes neither a business email nor phone. The unrelated email in embedded font metadata is not contact evidence.
- GoDaddy's form, tracking/cookie banner, repeated headings and generic promotional language were not reproduced. The new site has no tracking integration and no simulated submission.
- Original photo backgrounds were replaced with original vector graphics; they were not silently presented as project documentation.
- Original housing-insights.html in GitHub remains unchanged and available; this file was not part of the original pggllc.org content comparison.
- Domain/DNS/registration are unchanged. The cutover plan remains a future separately authorized step.
