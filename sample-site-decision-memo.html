Sample · Cedar Grove Community Trust

Retain land control. Authorize limited due diligence.

Decision requested
Authorize a bounded investigation of a hypothetical Colorado site for affordable rental housing. Do not authorize a land sale, development agreement, or construction commitment.
Preliminary recommendation
Advance conditionally. Continue only to resolve access, utility capacity, and the approval route. Do not assume a unit yield or a LIHTC award.
Illustrative site context
The nonprofit controls a hypothetical parcel near services. Affordable housing aligns with its mission. The zoning route and off-site infrastructure obligations remain unconfirmed.
Key unresolved issues
Written confirmation of allowed uses and review steps; utility provider capacity and connection requirements; preliminary access and environmental constraints; demand evidence and funding feasibility.
Authorized next actions
Staff may request a jurisdiction pre-application meeting and proposals from qualified civil, environmental, market, and financial specialists. Staff must return with scopes, costs, and decision gates before commissioning work outside existing authority.
Land-control protection
The nonprofit retains title and control. Any exclusivity, option, ground lease, disposition, or developer selection requires separate board review and legal advice.
Pause conditions
Pause if access cannot be resolved, infrastructure obligations exceed the board-approved budget, or the approval and funding paths do not support the mission. Revise the concept before extending the investigation.
Return to the board
Present confirmed findings, remaining uncertainties, consultant proposals, and a revised advance/revise/pause recommendation. The board separately approves any due-diligence budget and timeline.

Illustrative governance language only. Actual board resolutions and land-control documents require organization-specific review and legal advice.
