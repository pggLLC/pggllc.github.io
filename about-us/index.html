About pggLLC

A practical perspective on land, housing, and local government.

Paul Glasgow, AICP · Boulder, Colorado

Work that connects analysis to action.

pggLLC helps Colorado housing teams and landowners make informed site and entitlement decisions. Paul brings experience in municipal planning and development review, including service as Interim Planning & Development Director for the Town of Erie.

That public-sector perspective informs how the practice approaches development questions: understand the criteria, identify the missing evidence, and define a next step that fits the decision.

Paul’s professional profile

Advisory and development: clear choices.

Paul is a principal of both pggLLC and IndiBuild. pggLLC provides advisory services; IndiBuild is a separate affordable housing development partner.

You can use pggLLC advice with the developer you choose. An advisory inquiry does not commit you to an IndiBuild partnership.
