About pggLLC

The strength of a public and private perspective.

Planning, strategy, and design grounded in rigorous analysis, inclusive participation, and the practical work of implementation.

Paul Glasgow, AICP · Boulder, Colorado

Experienced in the process.
Focused on the outcome.

Paul brings comprehensive planning, public facilitation, land-use analysis, municipal department leadership, and affordable housing development experience to pggLLC. He has managed more than ten comprehensive plans and collaborated on additional plans with multidisciplinary teams. His University of Colorado research and teaching in planning methods, GIS, and urban design strengthen the analytical and educational expertise he brings to the practice.

pggLLC brings that breadth to municipalities, counties, and project teams: understand the evidence, engage the people affected, and build a path that works within local constraints.

How the practice works

Evidence. Participation. Implementation.

Rigorous analysis

Use public data, GIS, and policy analysis to make complex conditions legible and expose the assumptions behind a decision.

Public facilitation

Design and lead inclusive workshops, charrettes, and public conversations. Make complex choices understandable, explain trade-offs, and help participants establish shared priorities and decision criteria.

Implementable planning

Connect physical constraints, public priorities, economic conditions, and local operations to achievable next steps.
