Site & LIHTC Readiness Review

A better land decision starts before the application.

Find out which questions matter most before investing in a full development effort.

What the review covers

  • Site location, ownership context, land control, and development goals.
  • Zoning, allowed uses, density and the likely entitlement route.
  • Visible access, infrastructure, environmental and physical constraints to investigate.
  • Preliminary market context and potential funding alignment.
  • Key obstacles, unresolved assumptions, and recommended next investigations.

What you receive

A concise site decision memo with an advance, revise, or pause recommendation, supporting rationale, open questions, and a prioritized action list.

Preview a fictional memo

Bring what you have.

Start with the address or parcel number, available site information, land-control status, your intended residents, and the decision deadline. Missing information becomes part of the scope conversation.

Know the limits of an early screen

This is preliminary advisory work. A formal market study, survey, environmental review, engineering, cost estimate, legal analysis, and financial underwriting may be required later. LIHTC eligibility, competitiveness, and awards depend on current agency requirements and project-specific review; a readiness review promises no allocation or approval.
