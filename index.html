Site & LIHTC Readiness Review

A practical early screen that brings scattered questions into one decision.

What can the site support?

Screen zoning, land use, access, infrastructure questions, and visible site constraints. Identify what needs confirmation.

Where might the fit be?

Explore preliminary market context and funding alignment, including LIHTC considerations and the evidence still needed.

What should happen next?

Receive an advance, revise, or pause recommendation, key obstacles, and a prioritized due-diligence path.

Preliminary screening does not replace a formal market study, engineering, legal review, underwriting, or an agency funding determination. Scope and timing are agreed before work begins.
